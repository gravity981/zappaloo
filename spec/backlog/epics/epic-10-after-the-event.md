## Stories

### Story 10a – Export, shutdown and deletion

Export of all contributions; shutdown date with notice email; automatic deletion of all personal data; data minimisation reviewed. Mandatory, because the privacy notice promises the deletion.
Requirements: SEC-04, SEC-06, SEC-07, SEC-08.
